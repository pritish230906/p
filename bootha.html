Look closer at the low tide.

A pocket guide to the small, strange animals that live in the rock pools your feet skip past.

Meet the residents

Who you'll find

🦔

Purple sea urchin

Wedges itself into rock hollows and grazes on algae. Look for the neat, spiny circles at the pool's edge.

⭐

Ochre sea star

Orange to violet, and slow. It pries open mussels by pulling with thousands of tiny tube feet.

🦀

Hermit crab

Borrows an empty shell and upgrades as it grows. Stay still for a moment and it will start walking again.

Visit gently

  • Go within an hour of low tide.
  • Walk on bare rock, not on seaweed.
  • Touch with one wet finger, then let go.
  • Return every animal to where you found it.
